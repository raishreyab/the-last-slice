CHAPTER PROLOGUE

THE FORBIDDEN RITUAL

Many years ago, Pumpkin Crust was the town's most beloved Halloween pizzeria. Its eccentric owner, Elias, discovered an ancient magical Jack-o'-Lantern buried beneath the restaurant's foundations. Consumed by grief, he conducted a midnight ritual on Halloween night to recover someone he had lost forever.

THE TRAGEDY

TRANSFORMATION OF ELIAS

The ritual shattered. The primordial pumpkin curse backfired, warping Elias's flesh and soul into a towering, relentless Werewolf. The restaurant was sealed with heavy chains and padlocks. The employees vanished. But Elias remains inside, forever hunting in the dark corridors.

THE AWAKENING

YOUR LOST MEMORIES

You awaken inside the locked bedroom with no memory of how you arrived. A mysterious journal beside your bed delivers a single chilling warning: "Collect the pumpkin fragments before midnight. Whatever happens, DO NOT MAKE A SOUND."

GAMEPLAY FEATURES

🐺

THE BEAST REMEMBERS AI

Elias does not mindlessly wander. The Werewolf hears footstep frequencies, tracks sound hotspots, and remembers which wardrobes and lockers you repeatedly hide in, checking them more aggressively as chapters progress.

🎃

MAGICAL JACK-O'-LANTERN

Wield 4 supernatural abilities: Soul Light for dark areas, Ghost Vision to decode invisible occult runes, Flash Burst to temporarily blind and stun the beast, and Soul Tracker to summon guiding spirits.

🍕

9-ROOM INTERCONNECTED MAP

Explore a fully seamless pizzeria: Bedroom, Main Hallway, Haunted Kitchen, Dining Hall, Storage, Security CCTV Room, Owner's Office, the Subterranean Ritual Chamber, and the Main Exit. Previously unlocked doors remain accessible.

🧩

TACTILE SURVIVAL PUZZLES

Solve interactive 3D mysteries: rotary 3-digit combination safe dials, ritual pizza recipe assembly, dining table rune alignments, security circuit breaker switches, and ancient pentagram offerings.

🎧

PROCEDURAL WEB AUDIO

100% self-contained synthesized horror soundscapes: proximity heartbeats that accelerate from 60 to 150 BPM as Elias draws near, spatial floorboard creaks, distance-scaled growls, and bone-chilling howls.

📱

MOBILE & DESKTOP DUAL INPUT

Play on PC with smooth mouse-look and WASD controls, or on Android mobile browsers with custom virtual analog joystick, drag-to-look touch controls, and high-performance WebGL rendering.

SETTINGS

HOW TO SURVIVE

🎯 Objective: Explore the abandoned pizzeria, gather pumpkin fragments, solve mysteries, and avoid Elias the Werewolf.


🤫 Stealth & Noise:

• Crouching is nearly silent (Noise: 5).

• Running makes heavy sound (Noise: 45) and will attract Elias from afar!

• Creaking doors and dropped items make noise.

• Hide inside wardrobes and lockers when hunted.


🎃 Jack-o'-Lantern Abilities:

• [1] Soul Light: Illuminates dark corridors.

• [2] Ghost Vision: Reveals invisible runic clues on tables and walls.

• [3] Flash Burst: Blinds & stuns Elias for 5 seconds!

• [4] Soul Tracker: Wisps guide you to the nearest pumpkin shard.
